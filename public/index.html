A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Questions, answered.

Does everything work without an account? ＋

Yes. The browser-based tools can be used without signing in. Gnani transcription needs a configured API key on the app server.

Are routes guaranteed to be accessible? ＋

No. The route tool shows a basic route from the routing service. It does not currently verify wheelchair access, ramps, stairs, or surface conditions.

What happens to my photos or recordings? ＋

Photo text recognition runs in your browser. Audio is kept in your browser until you choose Transcribe. When configured, the selected audio is sent to Gnani for transcription.

What if a browser feature is not available? ＋

The tool will explain when a browser capability or permission is unavailable, and text-based controls remain usable wherever possible.
